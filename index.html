About

I am a second-year PhD student in Economics at the University of Wyoming, working in Behavioral, Environmental & Resource Economics. My research uses laboratory and survey experiments to study how information and policy design shape behavior in environmental settings: when people misreport, and whether a new option crowds out what people already do.

Research Interests

  • Behavioral and Experimental Economics
  • Environmental and Resource Economics
  • Information, Trust, and Policy Design

Research

Two experiments in progress: a laboratory study of audits and responsibility appeals in environmental subsidy reporting, and a survey experiment on whether buying real solar geoengineering credits crowds out carbon-offset purchases.
